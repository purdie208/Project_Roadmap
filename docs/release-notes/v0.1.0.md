The first release of **Roadmap**, a personal, offline roadmap for Windows. Use it to jot down ideas and features for your projects and follow them from idea to done. There's no account and no subscription, and nothing leaves your PC.

## Download

| File | Use it if… |
| --- | --- |
| **Roadmap-Setup-0.1.0.exe** | You want it installed with Start menu and desktop shortcuts. Recommended. |
| **Roadmap-Portable-0.1.0.exe** | You want a single file to run from anywhere without installing. |

> **Windows SmartScreen warning:** the app isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time you run it. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What's included

### Organize
- **Categories → Projects → Items.** Work and Personal are created for you. Add as many categories and projects as you like, and reorder them.
- **Color coding at every level.** Categories and projects have colors, and items inherit their project's color unless you give them their own. Choose from 12 presets or any custom color.
- **Statuses:** Idea, Planned, In progress, On hold and Done.
- **Priorities, tags, notes and checklists** on every item, with a progress bar for checklists.

### See your work
- **Board:** drag cards between status columns or reorder them. Drop a card on a project in the sidebar to move it there.
- **List:** a table you can sort by title, project, status, priority, deadline or last update.
- **Calendar:** a month view of deadlines. Click a day to add an item, or drag an item to another day to reschedule it.
- **Upcoming:** every deadline across all projects, grouped into overdue, today, tomorrow, the next 7 days and later.
- **Search** across titles, notes, tags, checklists, projects and categories.

### Deadlines and reminders
- Set a deadline with a date picker or a quick button (Today, Tomorrow, Next week, In a month).
- **Windows notifications** arrive before the deadline (from 15 minutes to 1 week ahead) and again when it's due. Click one to jump to the item.
- Closing the window keeps Roadmap running in the **system tray**, so reminders still go off.
- Optionally start Roadmap quietly when you sign in to Windows.

### Screenshots and images
- **Paste a screenshot** with Ctrl+V (capture one with Win+Shift+S). With an item open, it's attached to that item. With no item open, it creates a new idea from the screenshot.
- Drop image files onto a card or into the editor, or use **Browse…**.
- Click any image to view it full size, and use the arrow keys to flip between images.

### Look and feel
- **Light and dark themes** that follow your Windows setting by default. You can override this in Settings.
- Keyboard shortcuts: **Ctrl+N** creates an item, **Ctrl+F** opens search, **Ctrl+1/2/3** switches between Board, List and Calendar, and **Esc** closes whatever is open.
- Undo for deleted items, completed items and removed images.

### Your data
- Everything is stored locally in `%APPDATA%\Roadmap`. A safety copy is made each time the app starts.
- **Export and import backups** as a single file that includes your images.

## Known limitations
- Windows only for now.
- No sync between computers yet. It's planned for a future release.
- The app isn't code-signed yet (see the SmartScreen note above).
